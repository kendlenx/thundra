# THUNDRA Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2025-12-18  
Contact: kendlenx@gmail.com

THUNDRA is an iOS‑first lightning tracker built with a premium, dark UI. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## What We Collect
- **Location (with your permission):** Used to center the map, apply your alert radius, and determine nearby strikes. We do **not** collect or sell your precise location to third parties.
- **Lightning data:** The app uses a mock/local lightning feed for the current build. Future versions may use a real feed, but today all strikes are simulated or locally generated.
- **App settings:** Alert preferences (radius, time window, quiet hours) are stored locally on your device.
- **No personal account data:** We do not require sign-in and do not collect names, emails, or identifiers.
- **No analytics SDKs:** We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How We Use Data
- Show your position on the map (when allowed).
- Compute distance from strikes to decide if an alert should fire.
- Apply your configured alert radius, time window, and quiet hours.
- Improve app reliability (e.g., limit overlays and purge old strikes).

## Storage & Retention
- **Local only:** Strike records and alert settings are stored in a local database on your device.
- **Retention:** Strikes older than ~30 days are automatically purged to keep storage lean.
- **No cloud backup by us:** We do not upload your data to our servers. iCloud device backups (if enabled on your device) may include local app data per Apple’s behavior.

## Sharing
- We do **not** sell or share your personal data.
- **Map tiles/providers:** The app uses free dark tiles (OpenStreetMap/CARTO). Optional Google Maps (if you enable it) may receive standard map/telemetry requests per Google’s terms. We do not transmit your custom data to them.

## Notifications
- The app may show local notifications for nearby lightning when you enable alerts. Notifications are generated on-device based on your location and recent strikes. No push servers are involved.

## Children
- THUNDRA is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your Choices
- **Location:** You can allow or revoke location access in iOS Settings. If revoked, map centering and distance-based alerts are limited.
- **Notifications:** You can enable/disable alerts in-app and in iOS Settings.
- **Data removal:** To remove locally stored strikes/settings, uninstall the app. This deletes the local database.

## Future Data Sources
- If we add a real-time lightning provider in the future, we will update this policy to reflect any new data flows or providers. Paid APIs are not used in the current build.
