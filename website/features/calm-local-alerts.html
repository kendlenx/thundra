FEATURE GUIDE

Calm Local Alerts: warn early, avoid fatigue

THUNDRA alerting is designed to keep signal high and interruptions low. You can choose your own radius and quiet windows so notifications stay meaningful.

Download on App Store Back to homepage

Custom radius

Set a local distance threshold that matches your daily routine instead of receiving irrelevant alerts from far activity.

Quiet hours

Night windows reduce interruption while preserving awareness during active parts of the day.

On-device processing

Alerts are computed locally based on your settings and nearby strikes, without sending private behavior data to ad platforms.

Recommended setup

Urban daily mode: 15-25 km radius, quiet 23:00-07:00.
Outdoor day mode: 25-40 km radius, quiet off.
Storm week mode: tighter checks with frequent map review.
