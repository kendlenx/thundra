Lightning safety checklist for active storm days

Published March 4, 2026 · 5 minute read

Safety decisions are easier when you use a repeatable checklist. THUNDRA helps with awareness, but decision quality improves when your routine is clear.

Before leaving home

Check live strike map and nearby trend direction.
Set a temporary tighter alert radius for commute windows.
Review backup transport or indoor alternatives.

During outdoor activities

Pause activity if repeated nearby strikes appear.
Move to enclosed shelter rather than isolated structures.
Use alerts as early signal, not as the only safety trigger.

After the storm window

Review the timeline and identify missed warning cues.
Adjust quiet hours and radius for next similar pattern.
Share your setup with family members for consistency.
