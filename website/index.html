FAQ

Answers before the first storm.

Everything you need to know about THUNDRA's privacy, alerts, and daily use.

How does THUNDRA send alerts?

THUNDRA triggers local on-device notifications based on your alert radius and time window settings.

Does THUNDRA use my location?

THUNDRA only uses your location to center the map and calculate lightning distance for alerts.

Do I need an account?

No. THUNDRA does not require account creation to use core tracking and alert features.

Is THUNDRA ad-free?

Yes. THUNDRA is designed as a focused, ad-free experience.
